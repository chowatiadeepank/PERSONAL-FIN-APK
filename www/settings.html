Settings

Project

Personal Finance — your data is stored on this device's local storage by default. Nothing leaves this device unless you connect a Google Sheet below.

Display

Choose how the app lays itself out. "Auto" follows your screen size; the other two let you force a layout regardless of screen size.

Google Sheet sync (optional)

Paste the web app link from your own Google Apps Script project below. Once connected, every change you make here saves to that Google Sheet automatically in the background — no buttons to press. Opening the app on another device pulls the latest copy down automatically too.

Not connected — data stays on this device only.

Local backup file

Download everything as a single file you can keep safe or move to another device — works whether or not Google Sheet sync is on.

Restore

Load a previously exported backup file. This replaces all current data on this device.

Delete all data

Permanently erases every account, transaction, debt, investment, loan and recurring entry on this device. This cannot be undone. (If Google Sheet sync is on, it will be pushed there too.)
